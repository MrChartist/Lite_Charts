Fonts of this site

Type: Chartist Sans Flex (from Google Sans Flex by Google), Chartist Sans (from Plus Jakarta Sans by Tokotype) and
Chartist Serif (from Instrument Serif by Instrument), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Symbols that Chartist Sans
Flex lacks come from a small cut of Inter (The Inter Project Authors), also under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The files are the Mr. Chartist copies (subset and renamed, 2026), unchanged here. Each folder holds the original
OFL.txt with its copyright line. The fonts stay under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and are not sold on their own.
Source of these copies: src/assets/fonts/ of the Mr. Chartist app; licence notes: docs/theme/type/LICENSE.md.
