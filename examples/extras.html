DEMO · extras

createTooltip

Three looks for the values under the crosshair: follows the pointer, pins over the bar, or a strip along the top.

createWatermark

Text drawn on the canvas behind the series, so it is in every screenshot.

Fits the pane, any corner, opacity and size set by you.

createGoToRealtimeButton

Appears only while the newest bar is out of view, and scrolls back to it.

This chart starts scrolled into history.

createRangeSwitcher

A capsule radiogroup that sets the visible range by calendar span in the chart's time zone.

Arrow keys move and select; panning away clears it.

createPriceAlerts

Click the "+" by the price axis to add an alert line, drag it, remove it with the cross or Delete.

It fires once when an update crosses it.

No alert has fired yet.

createCompare

Other series as percent change from the first visible bar, re-based as you pan and zoom.

Each gets a theme colour and a chip in the legend.

createReplay

Pick a start bar, then play, pause, step and change speed; it only feeds setData and update.

"Live" leaves replay and shows every bar again.

createBands

A filled band between two line datasets, here Bollinger (20, 2), under the series.

Pass the chart to keep the whole band in autoscale.

Session breaks, partial line, vertical line

Day boundaries on intraday data in the chart's zone; a price line that starts at a bar; a labelled vertical line.
