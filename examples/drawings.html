DEMO · drawing tools

Pick a tool on the left, then click (or press, drag and release). A path ends with a double click or Enter. Shift keeps lines level or at 45 degrees; ctrl or cmd turns the magnet off for a point. Select a drawing to move it, drag its handles, style it, or press Delete. With the chart focused: Shift + H adds a horizontal line, arrows nudge the selected drawing, ctrl or cmd + Z undoes.
